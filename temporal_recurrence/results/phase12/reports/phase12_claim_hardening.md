## 2. Hardened Scientific Claims

1. **Duration-Dependent Degradation**: *"Continuous recurrent representations exhibit duration-dependent degradation in historical recoverability under conflicting distractor regimes."*
2. **Capacity Bottleneck**: *"Expanding recurrent state capacity ($d_m=64 \to 256$) did not fully eliminate duration-dependent degradation under the evaluated settings."*
3. **Exact vs. Structural Distinction**: *"Exact-edge memorization explains much of the signal when node pairs repeat; structural recurrence reveals historically predictive community invariants that persist even when exact edge overlap is suppressed."*
4. **Zero-Lag Recovery Scoping**: *"Under the tested recurrence protocol, MA-TGN restores evaluated predictive performance at recurrence onset ($k_A=0$) without requiring additional Regime $\mathcal{A}$ re-adaptation steps."*
5. **Real-World Scope**: *"Across the four evaluated recurrence episodes in SNAP CollegeMsg and SNAP Bitcoin-OTC, MA-TGN consistently outperforms Continuous TGN, providing supportive qualitative evidence."*
