# Phase 5 Literature Review: Positioning, Context & Prior Art

## 1. Executive Summary & Research Landscape
Temporal graph learning has rapidly expanded across continuous-time event modeling, discrete dynamic snapshots, and temporal link prediction. Existing methods largely partition into:
1. **Continuous Recurrent Memory GNNs** (e.g., TGN, JODIE, DyRep): Maintain per-node hidden states updated autoregressively upon each event.
2. **Memory-Free Temporal GNNs** (e.g., TGAT, CAW, GraphMixer, CRAFT): Rely exclusively on local causal neighborhood aggregation or target-aware attention over recent events.
3. **Exact Memorization Baselines** (e.g., EdgeBank): Maintain non-parametric hash sets of past observed edges.
4. **Graph Continual Learning** (e.g., ER-GNN, streaming GNNs): Study task-incremental distribution shift and multi-task forgetting using replay buffers.

Our work investigates an essential, unaddressed intersection: **What happens to continuous temporal representations when a previously observed dynamic regime recurs after a prolonged period of conflicting dynamics ($A \to B \to A$)?**

## 2. In-Depth Analysis of Mandatory Canonical Papers

### 1. "Towards Better Evaluation for Dynamic Link Prediction" (Poursafaei et al., NeurIPS 2022)
- **Key Findings**: Introduced the **EdgeBank** baseline, demonstrating that pure memorization of historical interacting pairs matches or outperforms complex state-of-the-art models on real-world temporal benchmarks due to high edge repetition and simplistic negative sampling.
- **Relation to Our Work**: EdgeBank serves as our primary exact-memorization baseline. 
- **Key Differences**:
  - EdgeBank performs deterministic binary lookup on past interacting pairs; it contains no mechanism to infer unobserved edges generated by latent community affinities.
  - Poursafaei et al. evaluated real datasets with uncontrolled, natural dynamics. We construct a mathematically controlled Dynamic Stochastic Block Model (DSBM) benchmark with independent orthogonal partitions to separate exact edge repetition from higher-order community structural recovery.
  - Poursafaei et al. did not parameterize distractor regime duration ($T_B$) or evaluate the degradation curve of recurrent state representations.

### 2. "Future Link Prediction Without Memory or Aggregation" (CRAFT, Yi et al., NeurIPS 2025)
- **Key Findings**: Introduced CRAFT, demonstrating that memory-free cross-attention between target nodes and recent interactions outperforms memory-based models while bypassing the computational bottleneck of sequential state updates.
- **Relation to Our Work**: CRAFT highlights the fragility and computational cost of online recurrent memory.
- **Key Differences**:
  - CRAFT addresses standard future link prediction where the immediate past is predictive of the near future, specifically aiming to improve performance on novel/unseen edges.
  - CRAFT does not study regime recurrence ($A \to B \to A$) or measure how information from distant regimes can be recovered. Memory-free models (including CRAFT and TGN-NoMemory) by design only attend to a local recent context window, forfeiting historical regime memory when $T_B$ exceeds the context window.
  - Our work does not argue that "memory is universally necessary"; rather, we diagnose why continuous recurrent state compression destroys historically predictive information when history is relevant.

### 3. Continuous-Time Memory GNNs: TGN, JODIE, DyRep
- **TGN** (Rossi et al., ICML 2020 W / arXiv 2020): Maintains persistent node memory updated via GRU cells on incoming events.
- **JODIE** (Kumar et al., KDD 2019): Projects user/item RNN embedding trajectories into future time steps.
- **DyRep** (Trivedi et al., ICLR 2019): Interleaves communication point processes with RNN state updates.
- **Relation & Differences**:
  - These architectures assume temporal continuity or smooth distribution shift.
  - Under non-stationary regime shifts with conflicting community structure ($A \to B \to A$), sequential RNN/GRU updates overwrite past regime states.
  - We show that this failure is fundamentally architectural: increasing recurrent state dimension ($d_m \in [16, 256]$) cannot overcome the destructive overwriting caused by long distractor regimes ($T_B \ge 100$).

### 4. Memory-Free & Sequence Models: TGAT, CAW, DyGFormer, GraphMixer
- **TGAT** (Xu et al., ICLR 2020) & **CAW** (Wang et al., ICLR 2021): Use temporal self-attention and causal anonymous walks over recent neighborhoods.
- **DyGFormer** (Yu et al., NeurIPS 2023) & **GraphMixer** (Cong et al., ICLR 2023): Use Transformer patchification or MLP mixers over recent co-occurrences.
- **Limitation under Recurrence**:
  - All these methods enforce a finite historical receptive field (typically $N \le 32$ or $64$ most recent interactions).
  - When a distractor regime $B$ spans $T_B > 64$ events, the relevant regime $A$ completely falls outside their receptive fields, reducing them to current-only prediction.

### 5. Graph Continual Learning & Catastrophic Forgetting
- **ER-GNN** (Zhou et al., IEEE TKDE 2021), **Streaming GNNs** (Wang et al., ICML 2024):
  - Study catastrophic forgetting across sequential graph tasks (typically task-incremental node classification across disjoint subsets of classes).
  - Use experience replay buffers or parameter regularization (EWC, MAS) to preserve past task accuracy.
- **Key Distinctions**:
  - Continual graph learning literature focuses on preventing forgetting during progressive task accumulation.
  - Our work focuses on continuous-time dynamic link prediction under temporal regime recurrence, parameterizing the distractor duration $T_B$ and measuring the recoverability of latent structural community distributions.

## 3. Systematic Literature Classification Summary

| Research Area | Canonical Representative | Mechanism | Handled Regime Recurrence? | Parametric vs Non-Parametric |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Recurrent Memory TGNNs** | TGN, JODIE, DyRep | Online GRU/RNN State Updates | **NO** (Overwritten during $B$) | Parametric ($d_m \in [16, 256]$) |
| **Local Temporal GNNs** | TGAT, CAW, GraphMixer | Recent Neighborhood Aggregation | **NO** (Truncated by Receptive Field) | Parametric / Feedforward |
| **Memory-Free Attention** | CRAFT (NeurIPS 2025) | Target Cross-Attention | **NO** (Only attends to local history) | Parametric Node Embeddings |
| **Exact Edge Memory** | EdgeBank (NeurIPS 2022) | Historical Pair Memorization | **PARTIAL** (Exact edges only, no community structure) | Non-Parametric Hash Table |
| **Graph Continual Learning** | ER-GNN, Streaming GNNs | Replay Buffers / Regularization | **NO** (Task-incremental, not continuous $T_B$ sweep) | Hybrid Buffer + Parametric |
| **Our Work** | **Temporal Recurrence Audit** | **Addressable Historical Snapshot Probe** | **YES** (Isolates $A \to B \to A$ recoverability) | Non-Parametric Snapshot Index |
