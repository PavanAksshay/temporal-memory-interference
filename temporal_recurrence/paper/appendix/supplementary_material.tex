\section{Exact Memory Accounting Derivations}
\label{app:memory_accounting}

For a graph with $N$ nodes, memory dimension $d_m$, key dimension $d_k$, and $K$ stored episodic checkpoints under 32-bit floating point precision ($4$ bytes per float):
\begin{align}
    \text{Continuous Node Memory} &= N \cdot d_m \cdot 4 \text{ bytes}, \\
    \text{Episodic Keys} &= K \cdot d_k \cdot 4 \text{ bytes}, \\
    \text{Episodic Values} &= K \cdot N \cdot d_m \cdot 4 \text{ bytes}, \\
    \text{Total RAM} &= \frac{4 (N d_m + K d_k + K N d_m)}{1024} \text{ KB}.
\end{align}

For the canonical configuration ($N=300, d_m=64, d_k=64, K=10$):
\begin{align}
    \text{Total RAM} &= \frac{4 (19,200 + 640 + 192,000)}{1024} = \mathbf{827.5\text{ KB}}.
\end{align}
